# Ioskeley Mono Term Nerd Font Mono

Unmodified Normal-width Regular and Bold fonts from Ioskeley Mono v2.2.0,
IoskeleyMono-Term-NerdFont.zip, Normal/ directory. Both report the family
IoskeleyMonoTerm Nerd Font Mono. No subsetting, renaming or glyph edits.
