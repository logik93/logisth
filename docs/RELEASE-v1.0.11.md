# logisth 1.0.11

- **Sampler → Stretch** brings all eight algorithms into one panel. Independent **ON** controls let the algorithms play in parallel with separate playback cursors.
- **PaulXStretch** adds **Stretch Amount** from 0.1x to 1024x and **FFT Size** from 256 to 262144 samples. The default is 2x with a 32768-sample FFT. The 0.5x, 1x and 2x ratio mixer remains available.
- The About panel and English manuals credit PaulStretch by Nasca Octavian Paul, PaulXStretch by Xenakios and Jesse Chappell, and Signalsmith Linear, whose FFT is used by the logisth implementation.
